Meta reads the ask.
The checker owns the number.

A shop names a part and everything it has to do. Muse Spark turns the typed brief into fields and proposes sizes and delivery moves. A deterministic checker scores every proposal across the delivery's whole range, weakest to strongest. The shop gets a build that holds or the exact change that makes the delivery usable — never a strength number written by a model.

01 · front door

Brief → state

Muse Spark writes down the product, how it is held, its size, its requirements and this delivery. Anything not said stays empty.

02 · propose

Spark proposes

Thickness, section, span, other product, blend. The exact proposed values are scored against every requirement; what does not pass goes back to Muse Spark once with the checked ranges.

03 · dispose

The checker decides

Every build is checked across the delivery's full range, all requirements at once, and the winner is the one using the least added material. No strength, stiffness or weight on the page is written by a model.
