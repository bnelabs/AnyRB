      Ask about this delivery

      01The part

      The part, its size, and what it has to do. Works for a solid rectangular section held in one of the three ways below. A size change may widen or lengthen a part, or change how it is held; it will not shrink the part you described. The ranges you enter are checked exactly as entered — no safety margin is added, and it certifies nothing.

      You do not need to know the stiffness number. Say what presses on it and how far it may bend down under that weight — the form works out the stiffness range from that. Only the lowest stiffness will matter unless you also give a smallest bend — without one, the form sets the high end at ten times the low end.

      Give the lowest it must reach and the highest it needs to reach — the whole delivery, weakest to strongest, has to sit between them. More than the high end is material you pay for without needing it; if only a minimum matters to you, set the high end well above anything you expect.

      02This delivery

      Every delivery of recycled plastic bends and breaks a little differently. Start from a published range for the kind of plastic — or use your supplier's numbers if you have them. Nobody is expected to know these by heart.

      Paste a supplier sheet and fill this delivery from it

      Paste the text your supplier sent. Only the plastic part is used, and only numbers actually in the text — anything missing stays empty for you to pick or type.

      Dirt and mix are written on the build ticket. They do not change any number here — your low end below should already reflect them.

      Have numbers from a supplier sheet or your own test? Enter them exactly

      Stiffness is how much the plastic bends. Strength is the load it takes before it breaks. “Low” is the weak end of this delivery — the part has to work even there, so that end sets the build. Your low end should already reflect the dirt and mix above.

      Or enter what the delivery measures after sorting or washing — from whoever does it, or your own test. The app does not guess this effect.

      03Share this delivery

      One delivery often has to cover more than one product. Pick another saved part, say how many of each are ordered, and the delivery above is shared between them — each checked against this delivery first.

      04Saved parts and deliveries

      Save a part or a delivery once, then pick it here next time instead of typing it again. Saved on this machine only.

      Settings

      Meta Model API is the built-in provider for this app. Nothing below needs changing to use it.

      Meta is the built-in choice: if the server already has a Meta key, you do not need your own. With any other provider, your key is needed — the server's key only works for Meta. Any key you enter is kept in this browser tab only, sent only to the server on this machine when the model is called, and never written into the page, the files, or this repository. It is gone when the tab closes.
