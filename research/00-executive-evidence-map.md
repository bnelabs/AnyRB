## Three sentences a reviewer should hold us to

1. **The variability is measured, and it is large** — the software's input distributions are literature-derived (graded, sourced, provisional), not measured for any specific supplier.
2. **The design loop is verified, not validated** — every claim is L0–L2; no physical test was performed, and the report says so on every page.
3. **The value is decision support, not certification** — the tool replaces design iteration and lot screening, and defines the minimum experiments that would upgrade its claims; it does not replace them.

## Assumptions that are explicitly flagged (not hidden)

- Lot-vs-within variance split (85/15 for E and σy) is evidence-*informed*, not a nested measurement; elongation/impact would use 60/40.
- Correlation between E and σy (0.50–0.60) has **no recyclate-specific measured source** — assumption, exposed as a sensitivity parameter.
- rPETG uses printed in-plane strength (46 MPa); out-of-plane/layer-normal loading is not modelled.
- The reference bracket is an illustrative product; the load case (150 N, 2.5 mm limit) is chosen to expose the design trade-off.
- The optimiser is a deterministic multi-start local search, not a proof of global optimality.
